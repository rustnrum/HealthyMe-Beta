# Healthy Me Beta v0.3.3

- Home Body Status hero now uses a calm mountain scene and opens a detailed Body Status explanation.
- Added Body Status detail view with issues, positive signals and suggested actions.
- Recovery is now calculated from sleep, cardio recovery and recent training load instead of duplicating Sleep.
- Recovery has a detailed contributor screen with score and confidence; Nutrition is N/A until Diet has real food data.
- Body tile color/status now follows weekly progress toward the user's weight goal instead of always appearing positive.
- Cardio tile includes respiratory rate when available and compares it with the user's baseline when enough history exists.
- Added Today's focus to Home for action-oriented suggestions.
- Persisted HRV and respiratory-rate histories from Health Connect for personal-baseline recovery calculations.
- Locked the future permanent beta signing/update-channel requirements in the product spec.
